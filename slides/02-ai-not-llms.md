## AI is not just LLMs

LLMs are one branch of a much larger field:

- **Computer Vision** - object detection, medical imaging
- **Robotics** - autonomous vehicles, manufacturing
- **Recommendation Systems** - Netflix, Spotify, ads
- **Classic ML** - fraud detection, forecasting, classification
- **Reinforcement Learning** - game playing, optimization

> LLMs dominate the headlines, but most deployed AI is not an LLM.
