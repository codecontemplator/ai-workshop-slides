## What is an LLM?

**Large Language Model** - a model that predicts the next most probable token.

Given: *"The cat sat on the"*

→ **mat** (72%) | carpet (8%) | floor (6%) | ...

### Key properties:

- Trained on internet-scale text (trillions of tokens)
- "Autocomplete on steroids"
- No explicit programming of rules - patterns emerge from data
- The "large" refers to billions of parameters (weights)
