## The Customization Spectrum

From easy/cheap → hard/expensive:

| # | Method | Effort | Cost |
|---|--------|--------|------|
| 1 | Prompt Engineering | Minutes | Free |
| 2 | Skills | Minutes | Free |
| 3 | Tool Use / MCP | Hours | Free/Cheap |
| 4 | RAG | Hours | Free/Cheap |
| 5 | Fine-tuning (LoRA) | Days | Moderate |
| 6 | Post-training (RLHF) | Weeks | Expensive |
| 7 | Full pre-training | Months | $$$$ |

Most real-world projects never go past #4.
